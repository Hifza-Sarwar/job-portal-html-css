Accounting

Job Description

Accounting jobs involve managing financial records, ensuring compliance, preparing statements (P&L, Balance Sheet), handling taxes, budgeting, analyzing data for business decisions, and auditing, with core duties

Resposibilities

  • Record, categorize, and manage all financial transactions (invoices, expenses, deposits).
  • Prepare and present financial statements (Balance Sheets, P&L, Cash Flow)..
  • Reconcile bank statements and general ledgers.
  • Compute and file tax returns, ensuring adherence to laws.
  • Analyze financial data to identify risks, recommend cost savings, and support decision-making..

Qualification

  • Accounting software (QuickBooks, SAP, etc.), Excel, GAAP/IFRS, Financial Analysis.
  • Accuracy, attention to detail, trustworthiness, organizational skills, problem-solving, communication, strategic thinking
  • Excellent communication (written/verbal).
