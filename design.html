Design

Job Description

Design and creative jobs involve conceptualizing visuals, creating graphics (logos, layouts, digital art) for print/web, ensuring brand consistency, and collaborating with teams

Resposibilities

  • Develop logos, layouts, images, illustrations, and promotional materials for digital and print.
  • Maintain brand identity and guidelines across all projects.
  • Work with marketing, product, and other teams to align visuals with goals.
  • Brainstorm and execute creative ideas that resonate with target audiences.
  • Tailor designs for different mediums (web, mobile, print).

Required Skills

  • Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), Sketch, Figma.
  • Typography, color theory, layout, branding.
  • Classroom management & organization.
  • Creative thinking, communication, attention to detail, time management, teamwork. .
