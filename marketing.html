Marketing

Job Description

Marketing job details involve developing and executing strategies to promote products/services, focusing on market research, content creation (digital/print), campaign management (social, paid ads), data analysis (ROI, trends), and collaborating across teams (sales, design) to boost brand awareness, generate leads, and drive sales, with roles varying from Marketing Assistant to Manager/Director.

Resposibilities

  • Analyze market trends, customer behavior, and competitor activities to define target audiences and strategies.
  • Develop comprehensive marketing plans, set goals, and determine the best channels (digital, print, events).
  • Write copy, design layouts, produce multimedia content, and manage content calendars.
  • Write copy, design layouts, produce multimedia content, and manage content calendars.
  • Track campaign performance, analyze data (conversion rates, traffic), and report on results to improve future efforts.

Required Skills

  • Analytical Skills (data interpretation)
  • Creativity & Communication (writing, design)
  • Digital Proficiency (SEO, social, CRM, analytics tools)
  • Project Management (meeting deadlines)
  • Adaptability (staying current with trends)
