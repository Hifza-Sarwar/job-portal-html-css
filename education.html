Education jobs vary widely, from classroom teaching (preschool to high school, special ed) focusing on lesson planning, student assessment, and parent communication, to support roles like school counseling (academic/career guidance), academic advising, and library management, plus administrative/specialized roles in curriculum design, educational technology, and instructional leadership,

Resposibilities

  • Develop curriculum, deliver lessons, manage classrooms, grade assignments, provide personalized support, communicate with parents, ensure safe learning environments..
